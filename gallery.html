Paintings by Malvika Kapoor

Original works by contemporary Indian artist Malvika Kapoor, shaped by 25+ years of practice and exhibited at Jehangir Art Gallery, Mumbai, and the Lalit Kala Akademi.

Most of these paintings are mixed media works on paper and board, built around colour and nature. Each one is an original.
