About the exhibition

Layers is a solo exhibition of eleven paintings by Malvika Kapoor at Jehangir Art Gallery in Mumbai, running from 27 October to 2 November 2026 in Exhibition Gallery 3. It is her third solo show at Jehangir Art Gallery, after 2012 and 2018.

Seen together, the series in Layers trace the artist's passage through time. Malvika is an abstract painter working chiefly in mixed media, and she has never kept to a single ground. Laminate, paper, canvas and, lately, black canvas each take pigment on their own terms, and her paintings begin in those unforeseen responses. The works in Layers are on paper, Canson sheet and canvas, and range from intimate 30 × 30 cm canvases to the 80 × 60 cm The Greens.

Nature, pattern and chikankari

Nature is her abiding source: gardens, trees and the quiet insistence of life unfolding around her. From these comes her saturated, unapologetic palette, with black, white and brown entering only to steady a composition. Before painting came fashion, and her early ambition to be a designer lives on in her instinct for pattern and in the way texture is draped across a surface.

Thirty years in Lucknow have also drawn chikankari into her studio. Murri, tepchi, ghas patti, shadow work and kangan stitches surface in her canvases as embroidered fragments set into paint, many cut from garments she wore for years. Sustainability has grown from that habit into a theme in its own right.

The artist

Malvika Kapoor

Malvika Kapoor completed her Bachelor's and Master's in Fine Arts in Painting at Banaras Hindu University in 1996. Marriage brought her to Lucknow that same year, and the city reshaped her practice, drawing her from the discipline of Banaras toward the texture, pattern and colour that now define her work.

She received the 25th State Lalit Kala Academy Award (Uttar Pradesh) in 2005, presented by Anjolie Ela Menon, and is a Working Artist at the Lalit Kala Akademi Regional Centre, Lucknow, and a member of the Bombay Art Society. She paints from her own atelier, the Malvika Kapoor Art Gallery in Lucknow, founded in 1997.
