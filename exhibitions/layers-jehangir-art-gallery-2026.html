The exhibition

“Each holds the state of mind that made it; together they form the account of a woman who, through all that life asked of her, kept returning to the canvas.”

From the Layers catalogue

Layers brought together eleven mixed media paintings on paper, Canson sheet and canvas, hung alongside the Balance, Concord, Elegance, Kin and Seasoned series. It was Malvika's third solo show at Jehangir Art Gallery, after 2012 and 2018.

Malvika is an abstract painter working chiefly in mixed media. Nature is her abiding source, and thirty years in Lucknow have drawn chikankari into the work: embroidered fragments set into paint, many cut from garments she wore for years.

Elegance I–VI

Chikankari as it was always worn, white on white, framed in red. Six works where the tradition is kept intact and only the setting has changed.

The artist

Malvika Kapoor

Trained in painting at Banaras Hindu University (BFA, MFA, 1996), Malvika has worked in Lucknow for three decades. She received the 25th State Lalit Kala Academy Award (U.P.) in 2005, presented by Anjolie Ela Menon, and is a member of the Bombay Art Society.

Her paintings are in the collections of the State Lalit Kala Academy, U.P., the Lalit Kala Akademi Regional Centre, Lucknow, and ICICI Bank, Lucknow, and in private collections in India, the USA, Dubai and the Netherlands.

Buying from Layers

Can I still buy a painting from Layers?

Yes. The eleven paintings are original works by Malvika Kapoor and can be bought directly from her. Message her on WhatsApp at +91 94509 06271.

How can I see a painting before buying?

Ask on WhatsApp and Malvika will send photos and video of the work, along with its size and price.

Why are prices not listed?

Prices are on request. Send the title of any painting and you will receive its price.

Does Malvika take commissions?

Yes. She takes commissions for paintings and custom work. Message her to discuss what you have in mind, or see the commissions page.

Where was the exhibition held?

In Exhibition Gallery 3 at Jehangir Art Gallery, Kala Ghoda, Mumbai, from 27 October to 2 November 2026.
