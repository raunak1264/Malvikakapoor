About Bloom 2

Bloom 2 is one of the paintings in Layers, Malvika Kapoor's solo exhibition at Jehangir Art Gallery, Mumbai (27 October – 2 November 2026). Like much of the work in the show, it is built up in mixed media, letting each surface take colour and texture on its own terms. Nature and pattern are Malvika's constant sources: gardens, trees and growth on one side, and on the other, textiles, embroidery and the eye of a designer.
